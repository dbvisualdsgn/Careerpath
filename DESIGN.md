# curriculi curricula: design system

A CV tool that reads like a page. Black and white, built from type, inspired by Kindle/Kobo
e-readers and Swiss editorial layouts. Three small accents (highlight, signal, go), no shadows, no gradients, and only the quiet motion described below.

## Brand

The brand is type only: **curriculi *curricula***, always lowercase, *curricula* in italics, set in the
sans, Switzer (`Wordmark`), whatever title font a visitor picks. There is no logo, mark, monogram or icon
next to it. On narrow screens the name
gets smaller (15px, 17px from `sm`), never hidden. The favicon is the letters "cc" in ink on paper.

## Colour tokens (`src/styles/theme.css`)

| Token | Default | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--paper` | `#ECEAE5` | Page background (e-ink paper), set by the Light panel |
| `--ink` | `#111111` | Text, rules that open a section, primary buttons |
| `--ink-soft` | computed | Secondary text, always ≥ 7:1 on the current paper (AAA) |
| `--rule` | computed | Hairlines between entries, field borders |
| `--focus` | ink | 2px focus outline, offset 2px |
| `--highlight` / `--signal` / `--go` | `#E6F05A` / `#E5482A` / `#2F8F5B` | Highlighter, error dot, passed check; text on them stays ink |

### Light (instead of dark mode)

There is no dark theme: a CV is a page to read and print. Like a Kobo, the **Light** panel (`PaperControls`,
`src/app/paper.ts`) has two sliders. **Warmth** moves the paper from cool white `#F5F6F3` through `#ECEAE5` to
warm `#ECDDBF`; **Brightness** dims it down to 80%. Ink stays `#111`, and the secondary greys are recomputed
for every setting, so all 441 combinations keep text contrast ≥ 7.2:1 (WCAG AAA). The choice is saved, applied
before first paint by an inline script, and used as the paper colour of the "As on the page" PDF.

Tailwind's `zinc-*` and `white` are remapped onto this warm neutral scale, and every `shadow-*` is
flat. Brand logos inside a CV are the only colour on screen (they belong to the CV, not the brand).

## Typography

**Titles** use any of the 78 Fontshare families that pass two checks: they have a 600–800 weight, and they
cover every letter the 7 interface languages need. Script and handwriting faces are excluded. Each is
shipped as **one bold file** (700, or 600/800 when there is no 700) registered for every weight, so
titles are always drawn in the real bold face. Tracking is near zero (`.font-title`) so bold letters
never touch. **Clash Display** is the default and is bundled with the CSS. Other families load on demand
(`src/app/titleFontLoader.ts`); the inline script in each page applies a saved choice before first paint.
The list is generated by `scripts/fontshare/fetch_title_fonts.py` into `src/app/titleFonts.ts` and
`public/fonts/titles/` (re-run it to refresh; credits in `public/fonts/titles/CREDITS.txt`).

**Text** is one of two faces, self-hosted from Fontshare (ITF Free Font License, `public/fonts/FONTSHARE.txt`):

| Choice | Family | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Sans | Switzer | Neutral Swiss grotesk |
| Serif (default) | Gambetta | Calm text serif, made for long reading |

The **Typefaces** menu (`TypeSwitcher`, the "T" button) has a searchable list of title fonts, each shown
in itself (previews load as rows scroll into view), and the Sans / Serif choice for text. The designed
PDF embeds the same title and text fonts.

- **Titles (`font-title`):** headings, names, job titles, big numbers.
- **Text (`font-body`):** summaries, bullets, descriptions, 16–20px, measure ≤60ch.
- **Micro labels and dates:** IBM Plex Mono 11–12px, uppercase, tracking 0.14em, `ink-soft`.
- **Brand and UI controls:** always Switzer, whatever title font is picked. The "ATS plain" PDF uses
  Helvetica (best for parsing).

## Patterns

- **Section opener:** 1px ink rule and a mono title (`SectionHead`). No running numbers on the CV.
- **CV sheet:** the CV sits on one sheet (`panel`, a shade lighter than the `paper` desk) with a hairline
  edge and a soft shadow. Print drops the sheet. Next to the name, on the left, the person picks nothing,
  their initials (a black square) or a photo (square, no rounding, grayscale, 480px). The PDF follows the same choice.
- **CV page bars:** row 1 is the site (back, wordmark, My CVs, Public CVs). Row 2 is the CV menu, everything
  you can do with this CV: Edit, Recruiter check, Skim view | typefaces, language, light | Private/Public,
  Share, Download, Delete. Labels show from `md`; below that the buttons are icons with names for screen readers.
- **Recruiter check on the page:** the black frame shows only while "Recruiter check" is on in the CV menu.
  "See the full check" opens the job-ad match and every tip under it. The editor holds only the CV's content.
- **Summary panel:** the summary sits in a hairline box on `paper`, the only tinted block on the sheet.
- **Skills:** core skills and tools side by side in 2 columns from `md`, stacked on phones.
- **Ruled lists:** entries separated by `rule` hairlines (experience, education, FAQ).
- **Hairline grids:** `gap-px` on a `rule` background (steps, tools, languages).
- **Buttons:** primary = ink fill / paper text; secondary = 1px ink outline; tertiary = underlined text link.
- **Button states (everywhere):** hover = outline fills with ink (or a tint for ghost buttons); pressed = 1px nudge
  down; selected (`aria-pressed`, open menu) = ink fill; focus = 2px ink outline, offset 2px; disabled = 50% opacity.
- **Segmented tabs:** one hairline box divided by ink rules. Inactive = soft text, hover = tint + ink text,
  selected = ink fill with paper text, focus = inset outline.
- **Editor sheet:** same paper as the page with a hairline edge; an ink header band is what marks it as the tool,
  not part of the CV.
- **Recruiter-check frames:** every recruiter-check surface (the card above the CV, the score card in the
  editor, the homepage infographic, the hero pop-over) sits on black via `.on-ink`, which flips the colour
  tokens inside the frame. Text stays AAA (light ink 16:1, soft 9:1) and the yellow highlight keeps ink text.
- **ATS score mark:** dashed outline (<65), outline (65–84), filled (≥85), always with the number.
- **Icons:** Lucide only, 16–22px, stroke 1.5–2, always `aria-hidden` next to a text label.

## Motion

Motion is allowed only where it helps reading, and it never hides content for long:
- **One reveal per section:** a section fades and rises 8px once, the first time it scrolls into view
  (`useReveal` + `.reveal`). A timer shows it anyway after 2.5s.
- **Count-up on scores:** score numbers count up once (`useCountUp`), about 0.7–1.1s.
- **Small details:** the highlighter stroke draws in, score bars grow, arrows nudge 2–3px on hover,
  a black dot travels the privacy diagram (to "Saved here" for Private, along the whole link for
  Public), the hero caret blinks, switches slide.
- **Example CV gallery (homepage):** two rows of example CVs drift slowly in opposite directions (90s per
  loop). They stop on hover, on keyboard focus and with the Pause button (WCAG 2.2.2).
- **Hero cards:** 5 short CVs of people from pre-euro banknotes (Marie Curie, Carl Friedrich Gauss,
  Alessandro Volta, Adolphe Sax, Gustave Eiffel), one at a time (`src/home/PageSpecimen.tsx`). Each sits on a
  paper from the site's light scale (cool to warm) or on the highlight yellow, and has a title font chosen for the person; types itself, then the recruiter check pops over
  it; after a hold the next one comes. Previous, Pause and Next buttons sit under the card (WCAG 2.2.2). Under
  reduced motion each card shows finished and nothing rotates. Ink is always #111111 and soft text comes
  from the paper scale (7:1 or more).
- **Why page documents:** Amara Okafor (an example CV) has a passport, a driving licence and a CV
  (`src/why/Documents.tsx`). In a 7s loop a plane flies to Rome and a yellow entry stamp lands, a car
  drives to the hospital and it lights up, a result on the CV is highlighted and an interview invitation
  appears. The resting state is the end of each story, which is what reduced motion shows. A ticker of the
  same words drifts above them. One Pause button stops both (WCAG 2.2.2).
- **Example CV cards** (homepage gallery, /people/) are small sheets in the current template
  (`src/app/components/CVCard.tsx`): panel paper, avatar on the left or nothing. About 60% of the examples
  show nothing next to the name, 30% initials and 10% a photo (`src/app/cv/people.ts`).
- **Accents in motion:** only ink (black) and the highlight yellow move or animate; signal orange is kept
  for static error marks.
- **Reduced motion:** with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, all of it is off. Sections, highlights and bars
  are shown at once, numbers show their final value, nothing loops, and the gallery is a still row
  you can scroll.

No page transitions, parallax, scroll-jacking or motion that carries meaning on its own.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA with zero axe-core violations, plus AAA text contrast (axe `color-contrast-enhanced`),
at every Light setting. Includes a skip link, landmarks, a logical heading order, a native radio group
for the type switch, visible focus, and motion that switches off under reduced motion.
